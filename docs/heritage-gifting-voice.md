# Heritage Gifting: copy voice

Applies to every Heritage Gifting page (starting with Vaazhga Vaazhthudan reports). Approved by the founder.

- **Speaker:** "we", meaning Distant Devotion. Never a named representative.
- **Reader:** the person who sent the blessing, addressed as "you". They are who we are accountable to, and they share the page onward as a gift.
- **Tone:** warm and emotional, with blessing and legacy first. Never transactional.
- **Say:** "send a blessing", "gift", "remembered".
- **Avoid:** book, order, package, customer, service, "said aloud", "lit". No programme name in the copy.
- **Master tagline:** Connecting Hearts and Roots.

## Approved lines

- Hero heading: **Done, as promised** (bold Cal Sans).
- Hero story: "You asked us to remember Akshanth before Swamimalai Murugan. We did, with his name, his star, and your love for him. Wherever he is today, a blessing found him."
- Other approved story options: "This morning, at Swamimalai Murugan temple, Akshanth's name was carried to the Lord with your wish for his long, happy life. Nothing was left out. Wherever he is today, he was remembered here." and "At Swamimalai Murugan temple, Akshanth's name reached the Lord's feet this morning, along with every wish you sent for him. It is done, and it was done with care."
- CTA: **Send to Akshanth**.

## Brand

Maroon `#3A0B1B`, orange `#FF4E00`, ivory/white from the logo. Cal Sans headings, Inter body, Noto Sans Tamil for Tamil.
